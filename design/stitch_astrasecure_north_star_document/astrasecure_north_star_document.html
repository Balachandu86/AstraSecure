# AstraSecure – UI/UX Philosophy & Screen Architecture ## 1. Product Nature A secure mission communication and coordination tool for controlled access environments. - **Tone:** Tactical, Minimal, Secure, Structured, Quiet, Operational. - **Feel:** Purpose-driven, information-dense, professional. No social media aesthetics. ## 2. Core UX Principles - **Security-First:** Visible encryption status, session status, and clear identity/clearance. - **Compartmentalization:** Users only see what they are cleared for. - **Low Cognitive Load:** No stories, reactions, or stickers. Focus on channels, messages, and files. - **Fast Navigation:** Mission → Channel → Conversation (≤ 3 taps). ## 3. Screen Architecture 1. **Missions List (Home):** Entry point showing authorized operational workspaces. 2. **Mission Channel List:** Nested hierarchy (Announcements, Command, Logistics, Recon, etc.). 3. **Channel Chat:** Secure messaging with encryption indicators and transformation tools. 4. **Members / Roles:** Permission-based visibility of callsigns and ranks. 5. **Files / Documents:** Mission-scoped encrypted storage. 6. **Secure Tools:** Tactical toolkit (Encode/Decode, Hash, Base64, XOR). 7. **Settings & Security:** Device ID, key rotation, identity export, and session status. 8. **Panic / Secure Wipe:** Highly intentional, serious, and irreversible action screen. ## 4. Visual Direction - **Colors:** Dark theme primary, muted tones, role-based accents, functional status colors (G/A/R). - **Typography:** Clean sans-serif, high density, readable in low light. - **Icons:** Minimal, symbolic (locks, shields, keys), functional. ## 5. Interaction Rules - Unauthorized content is hidden (not just locked). - System events (key rotation, member removal) are subtle but visible. - Auto-lock and screenshot protection are core features.
